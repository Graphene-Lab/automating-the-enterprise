## What this book does not promise

This book describes a method and a set of tools. It does not promise a particular financial
result, and it does not constitute accounting, tax or legal advice. Every company's circumstances
differ; the configuration that suits a wholesaler in one country may be wrong for a manufacturer in
another, and the tax rules that apply to you are the ones your tax office publishes, not the ones
in a book.

Before changing how your company keeps its records, take advice from your accountant. Before
automating an operation that moves money, make sure you can reverse it. Before giving an assistant
permission to write to your ERP, decide what it must never be allowed to do, and configure that
limit first.

## Versions

The software versions described in this book are those that were current when the book was
written: Odoo Community 20, PostgreSQL 16, AgentBridge and OdooTool as released in late 2026. All
of these projects change. Where a screen in your installation does not match a screenshot in this
book, assume the software is newer and look for the same setting under a slightly different name;
the model, the documents and the rules change far more slowly than the buttons.
